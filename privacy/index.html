Privacy Policy

In plain English. Last updated 19 May 2026.

The short version

We collect the minimum we need to run a small AI class business. We don't sell your data. We don't run ads. You can ask us to delete everything we hold about you at any time by emailing hello@takeawai.com.

Who we are

TakeawAI is a small online education business run by Michael Marciano in the United Kingdom. Contact: hello@takeawai.com.

For the purposes of UK GDPR, the data controller is Michael Marciano trading as TakeawAI.

What we collect

We only collect personal data when you give it to us. Specifically:

If you subscribe to the newsletter: your email address (handled by Substack — see their privacy policy at substack.com/privacy).

If you fill in the contact form: your name, email address, the subject you select, and the message you send.

If you enrol in a class: your name, email address, the cohort you book, and any payment is handled by a third-party payment provider (we never see or store your card details).

We do not currently use analytics cookies. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this page first.

Why we collect it

To send you the newsletter you signed up for. To reply to messages you sent. To run the classes you booked. That's it.

Who we share it with

We don't sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers. We do use a small number of trusted providers to actually deliver the service:

Substack — newsletter delivery and subscriber management.

A payment provider — to take payment for the £299 course. They process your card details directly; we don't store them.

If a UK authority makes a lawful request for data, we will comply with the law.

How long we keep it

Newsletter subscribers: until you unsubscribe. There's a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom of every issue.

Contact form messages: kept for up to 2 years so we can refer back if you write again, then deleted.

Class enrolments: kept for up to 6 years (UK tax records requirement), then deleted.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

Ask what data we hold about you. Ask us to correct it. Ask us to delete it. Ask us to stop sending you marketing emails (unsubscribe link does this). Object to how we use your data. Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

To do any of the above, email hello@takeawai.com. We aim to respond within 7 days.

Children

TakeawAI is designed for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you think we have, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything material, we'll update the date at the top and tell newsletter subscribers in the next issue.

Got a question about your data? Email hello@takeawai.com. Real person, real reply, usually within a day or two.
